Description

A healthy and high-protein recipe for those who want to eat something delicious and good for your health

Ingredients

  • 300g oat
  • 1 tea spoon of baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 100ml milk
  • 50g chocolate chips
  • 1 tea spoon of honey

Steps

  1. Mix the oat, eggs, baking powder, honey and milk in a bowl
  2. In a hot oven, cook the blend
  3. Enjoy
