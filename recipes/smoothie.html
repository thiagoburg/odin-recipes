Smoothie

Home

Description

A healthy and high-protein recipe for those who want to eat something delicious and good for your health

Ingredients

  • 300g oat
  • 1 banana
  • 2 eggs
  • 300ml milk
  • 1 tea spoon of honey

Steps

  1. Mix the oat, eggs, banana, honey and milk with a blender
  2. In a hot saucepan cook the blend
  3. Enjoy
