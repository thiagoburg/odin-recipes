Description

A healthy and high-protein recipe for those who want to eat something delicious and good for your health

Ingredients

  • 100g oat
  • 2 bananas
  • 50g chocolate-flavoured protein powder
  • 500ml milk
  • 1 tea spoon of honey

Steps

  1. Mix the oat, bananas, honey, protein powder and milk with a blender
  2. Enjoy
